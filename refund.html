2. SUBSCRIPTION CHARGES

The App may offer paid subscription plans. The applicable plan, price, billing period, renewal terms and included features will be displayed before purchase. Charges are payable through the payment method or platform presented at checkout.

3. REFUND ELIGIBILITY

A refund request may be considered where the same subscription payment was collected more than once; payment was deducted but the subscription was not activated; an incorrect amount was charged due to a verified billing error; or a refund is required by applicable law or payment-platform rules. Each request will be reviewed against the transaction record and supporting information.

4. NON-REFUNDABLE CIRCUMSTANCES

Except where required by applicable law or approved by us, refunds may not be available for a subscription period already used, partial use of an active subscription period, failure to use the App after successful activation, change of mind after activation, or account suspension resulting from breach of the Terms and Conditions. Nothing in this section limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

5. CANCELLATION AND AUTO-RENEWAL

You may cancel a subscription using the cancellation facility provided by the platform through which you purchased it, or by contacting our support team where applicable. Cancellation generally prevents the next renewal; it does not automatically refund the current subscription period. Unless purchase terms state otherwise, access may continue until the end of the paid period. You remain responsible for charges already due.

If the subscription was purchased through Google Play, manage cancellation through the Google Play account used for the purchase. Uninstalling the App does not itself cancel a subscription.

6. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

Contact support and include your registered name and account details, subscription order or transaction ID, date and amount of payment, subscription plan if known, reason for the request, and payment receipt or supporting evidence. Do not send passwords, UPI PINs, card PINs or one-time passwords.

7. REVIEW AND REFUND PROCESSING

We will review the request and may ask for additional information. If approved, a refund will normally be initiated within 7–10 business days after approval. The time for funds to appear depends on the bank, payment provider or payment platform.

For purchases made through Google Play or another third-party platform, that platform's refund rules and process may apply. You may need to submit the request directly through that platform. Approval is subject to applicable platform rules and law.

8. FAILED OR PENDING PAYMENTS

If money is deducted but the subscription is not activated, contact us with the transaction details. We will check the payment status and, where appropriate, arrange or support a reversal or refund.

9. POLICY CHANGES

We may update this Policy. The latest version will be made available through the App or an official website.
