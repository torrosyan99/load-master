This Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use the App.

2. INFORMATION THAT MAY BE PROCESSED

The information processed depends on the features enabled and how the App is used.

A. Account information: name and mobile number; email address if provided; login and authentication information; user role and account permissions.

B. Driver and truck information: driver names and contact details; truck registration numbers and vehicle details; driver-to-truck assignments and operational records.

C. Trip and business information: trip dates and details; material, quantity and transportation details; loading and unloading locations entered by users; waybill details and reference numbers; customer and supplier information.

D. Financial and operational information: driver advances and trip earnings; payment and settlement records; fuel and other operational expenses; outstanding balances and business summaries; subscription and transaction details needed to manage paid access.

E. Technical information: the App or its hosting and diagnostic services may process device type, operating system, App version, IP address, crash reports and performance logs.

F. Device location: GSM Suppliers Trips is not intended to collect GPS or device location in the current version. Location details such as loading and unloading locations may be entered by users as trip information. If a future version introduces a location-based feature, this Policy will be updated and relevant device permission will be requested where required.

3. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Information may be used to create and manage accounts and permissions; maintain truck, driver, trip and waybill records; record advances, payments, settlements and expenses; generate reports; provide support; manage subscriptions and billing enquiries; maintain security; troubleshoot and improve the App; meet legal obligations; and address disputes.

4. HOW INFORMATION MAY BE SHARED

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared when necessary with authorised users within the relevant business account; hosting, cloud and technical service providers; payment platforms or payment service providers to process or verify subscription transactions; authorities when legally required; or to protect rights, safety and security as permitted by law.

5. SUBSCRIPTION AND PAYMENT INFORMATION

If you purchase a subscription, payment details may be processed by the payment provider or platform used. We may receive limited transaction information, such as order reference, payment status, subscription plan and billing dates, to activate and manage your subscription. We do not intend to collect or store complete card details, UPI PINs, passwords or one-time passwords.

6. DATA SECURITY

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices and login credentials and must not share passwords or one-time passwords.

7. DATA RETENTION

Information may be retained as long as necessary to provide the App, manage subscriptions, maintain operational and financial records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect legitimate business interests. When information is no longer required, appropriate steps will be taken to delete or anonymise it, subject to legal retention requirements and technical limitations.

8. YOUR REQUESTS AND CHOICES

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of eligible personal information, or raise a concern about its processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent. Withdrawal may affect features that depend on the relevant information. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

9. CHILDREN

The App is intended for business and operational use and is not designed for children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the App.

10. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The App may use third-party hosting, authentication, notifications, analytics or payment services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy terms and applicable agreements.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Policy. The latest version will be made available through the App or an official website, with the updated date shown above.
