Yes. Customer setup includes GST and Non-GST types, and the trip list separates these records. The app also includes invoice views, bills of supply, GST statements, purchase bills and an E-Way Bill section.

Administrators add supervisors with phone and PIN access and select the modules they can open. Driver login is optional and lets a driver view assigned trips on their own phone.
